# Surface brief: Home (welcome) page

Scope: the page that replaces the Main Menu for signed-in users and greets signed-out visitors before sign-in. Mode: Persuade (introduce the app; for visitors the action is Sign in / Create account). Rest of the app keeps its white-card coral world.

Audience/job: women who keep symptothermal observations (cs, sk, uk, en), mostly on a phone. A first-time visitor learns what the app is and signs in; a signed-in user lands on a friendly home that also announces what is coming (who makes it, why, a step-by-step guide article, courses). No content exists yet: those four are honest "coming soon" placeholders, never invented text or links. No navigation tiles back into the app (user decision); the header and phone tab bar navigate.

Constraints: educational framing only, a footer line saying the app is not medical advice; no fertility or contraception claims in copy or art; flowers carry no data meaning. All strings in cs/sk/uk. Works at 320 px; reduced motion keeps the meadow still.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is standing in a gerbera meadow: the first viewport is a pink sky over three layered hills of hand-drawn gerberas, and the content grows out of the meadow like garden signs. Refuses the category's phone-mockup hero over a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Flat sky blush #fbe7ea; hills in three sage steps (#dbe8d0, #cfe0c3, #b5cfa8); gerberas with slim petals in peach #f6a98d, rose #f19bb0, butter #f6d27a, lavender #c8b6e8 and white, cocoa-and-butter centres; ink #33242b, muted #6a4f58; coral only on the one action. Rounded friendly display face (Rubik, self-hosted, Cyrillic-capable) over Inter body. Sections below sit on meadow-green ground as white rounded garden-sign cards on short stems; coming-soon signs are dashed and carry a "Brzy" tag. Flat colour only, no gradients.

STORY: The visitor sees the meadow, reads one line about recording a cycle day by day, understands she owns the interpretation, signs in (or, signed in, scrolls to what is coming).

FIRST VIEWPORT (phone): small brand line top-left with sign-in pill top-right (visitors); 38px headline in the sky, one supporting sentence, coral "Sign in" pill (visitors) or a greeting (signed in); the lower ~55% is the meadow, foreground flowers largest. Desktop: same scene wider, headline left, meadow full width.
